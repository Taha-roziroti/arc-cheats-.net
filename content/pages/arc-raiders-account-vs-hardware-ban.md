# Arc Raiders Account Ban vs. Hardware Ban

Third-party tools can violate Embark Studios terms. This article explains the difference between account-level and hardware-level enforcement in plain language—it is not legal advice and does not quote unpublished error codes.

![Arc Raiders account ban versus hardware ban comparison](/images/showcase/arc-gameplay-outdoor.webp)

## What is an Arc Raiders account ban?

An account ban typically blocks the signed-in profile from matchmaking or the live service. Recovery paths depend on publisher policy and whether the ban is permanent. We do not operate Embark support queues.

## What is an Arc Raiders hardware ban?

A hardware ban ties enforcement to device identifiers. A new account on the same machine may still fail to connect. Vendor claims about “HWID protection” belong in product docs we have not verified on this domain—see excluded utilities in our site plan.
